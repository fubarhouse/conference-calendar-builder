Usage Instructions

  • Browse and filter sessions and events to find the ones you want to attend.
  • Open a session card for details, and use the checkbox on each card to quickly select sessions. Your selections are automatically saved to your browser.
  • Use the download button of prefernce to download your personalized schedule and import it into your calendar application.
